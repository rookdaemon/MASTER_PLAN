# Decision 0002: Gate self-replicating conscious infrastructure on coexistence governance

## Context

The existing plan protects welfare and delays self-replication behind scientific and institutional
gates, but it does not explicitly test coercive preservation, ecological boundary ratchets,
infrastructure chokepoints, identity manipulation through copying, or biosphere externalities.
The machine-ecology thought experiment exposed this missing bridge between durable engineering and
legitimate coexistence.

## Decision

Add `capability-cross-substrate-coexistence-governance` to the institutional-continuity portfolio.
Its metric requires two independently reviewed external governance exercises. Make verification of
that capability an activation gate and dependency of `program-self-replication`.

The governing doctrine is [`docs/machine-ecology-and-coexistence.md`](../../docs/machine-ecology-and-coexistence.md).
It challenges both survival-only optimization and the proposed replacement objective; the plan uses
non-substitutable constraints and a tradeoff ledger instead of a single scalar objective.

## Alternatives rejected

1. **Adopt “no substrate class may dominate another” verbatim.** Rejected because substrate classes
   are not political agents and parity can miss actual coercion, ownership, and chokepoints.
2. **Treat ecological zoning as the expected solution.** Rejected because zoning can encode forced
   segregation or expand through a contamination rationale without consent or redress.
3. **Rely on the existing welfare gate.** Rejected because welfare assessment does not cover agency,
   political power, identity topology, environmental externalities, or institutional contestability.
4. **Amend G1-G3.** Not necessary for this bounded change. The new gate operationalizes existing
   preservation priority, welfare precaution, and institutional-continuity commitments.

## Consequences

- Self-replicating conscious infrastructure cannot advance on engineering readiness alone.
- Local artifacts remain planning evidence only; they cannot increment the metric.
- External reviews must cover the specified adversarial scenarios and produce auditable evidence.
- A future change that alters G1-G3 or an ethical invariant still requires the constitutional
  amendment procedure.
