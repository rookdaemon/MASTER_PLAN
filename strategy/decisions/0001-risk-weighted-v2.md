# Decision 0001: Replace the technology-first execution order

Status: human review required
Recorded: 2026-08-03T00:00:00.000Z

## Context

The frozen v1 roadmap maps a technology ladder but frequently conflates repository completion
with scientific or real-world attainment. The previous continuous maintainer produced recursive
decomposition and document churn without a convergence criterion.

## Decision

Retain G1–G3 and the ethical invariants as the constitutional core. Treat strategies,
hypotheses, priorities, and decomposition as revisable. Allocate the bootstrap portfolio 35%
to consciousness epistemics, 30% to near-term preservation, 20% to enabling capabilities, and
15% to institutional continuity. Keep space settlement, self-replication, and cosmological
engineering visible but ineligible until their activation gates are verified.

Only one bounded work packet may be active. Evidence-bearing positive, negative, or null results
are integrated; document completion alone never verifies an outcome.

## Objections considered

- Deferring expansion could delay long-horizon resilience work.
- Consciousness epistemics may remain underdetermined despite substantial effort.
- Fixed portfolio targets can become stale as evidence changes.

## Consequences

- Portfolio weights receive weekly human review and quarterly formal review.
- Expansion work remains mapped and can activate when precursor evidence becomes credible.
- The v1 plan is preserved unchanged and audited as historical evidence input.
- This decision and all strategy/governance changes require a human-reviewed pull request.
