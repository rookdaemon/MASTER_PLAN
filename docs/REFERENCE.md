# MASTER_PLAN Reference Map

## How to read the references

The [canonical plan](PLAN.md) states what the project is trying to do. The
[operating model](OPERATIONS.md) states how decisions and work are controlled. This reference layer
retains deeper findings, designs, protocols, and open questions without presenting them as a second
plan.

Every dossier uses four epistemic labels:

- **Implemented** — behavior exists in repository source and is covered by tests; this says nothing
  by itself about external validity.
- **Observed** — a bounded experiment, source review, or analysis produced a recorded result with
  stated limitations.
- **Proposed** — a design, protocol, or research program exists but has not met its external outcome
  contract.
- **Speculative** — a long-horizon possibility remains worth tracking but lacks adequate evidence or
  near-term eligibility.

## The seven dossiers

1. [Consciousness science](reference/consciousness-science.md) — theories, neural correlates,
   predictions, indicators, metrics, reports, and substrate-independence tests.
2. [Ethics and coexistence](reference/ethics-and-coexistence.md) — moral foundations, objections,
   welfare, legal standing, creation safeguards, rights, and cross-substrate governance.
3. [Agents and societies](reference/agents-and-societies.md) — cognitive modules, agent runtime,
   embodiment, autonomy, social cognition, culture, and simulation.
4. [Continuity and identity](reference/continuity-and-identity.md) — emulation, scanning, transfer,
   replacement, identity topology, redundancy, and graceful degradation.
5. [Durable infrastructure](reference/durable-infrastructure.md) — radiation tolerance, repair,
   energy, manufacturing, fault models, and self-maintaining computation.
6. [Space and longevity](reference/space-and-longevity.md) — habitats, resources, propulsion,
   replication, colonies, distributed networks, stellar engineering, and cosmology.
7. [Institutions and transmission](reference/institutions-and-transmission.md) — doctrine,
   education, narrative, practice, identity, governance, funding, succession, and preservation of
   knowledge.

## Source and evidence discipline

References retain useful reasoning and citations while removing duplicated card workflow,
repository-completion claims, and procedural decomposition. A proposal remains a proposal even when
its document is polished. A software test establishes behavior under its fixtures, not consciousness,
physical durability, institutional readiness, or civilizational capability.

Machine-readable evidence and consolidated findings live under `strategy/`. Claims that affect
priorities or lifecycle state must be represented there and pass the relevant outcome contract;
reference prose alone cannot activate work.
