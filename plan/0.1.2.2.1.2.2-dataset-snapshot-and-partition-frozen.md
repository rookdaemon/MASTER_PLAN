## Description

Archive the permitted calibration dataset as an immutable snapshot and execute the prespecified partition procedure without inspecting held-out outcomes. Publish a machine-readable manifest that assigns grouped `SystemModel` identifiers to calibration or held-out data and records the snapshot identifier, split configuration, assignments, and content hashes.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The dataset snapshot has a durable immutable identifier and verified content hash
- The partition manifest records the snapshot identifier, grouping and stratification variables, ratio, seed, every grouped identifier assignment, and its own content hash
- Automated validation confirms complete assignment, group isolation, identifier non-overlap, and absence of held-out outcome access
