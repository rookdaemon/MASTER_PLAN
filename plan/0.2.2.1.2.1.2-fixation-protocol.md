# 0.2.2.1.2.1.2 Fixation Protocol [PLAN]

## Description

Design and qualify a reproducible whole-brain fixation or vitrification protocol that meets the declared L2+ preservation requirements.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Reagents, equipment, control parameters, sampling locations, timing boundaries, and failure-abort conditions are specified.
- Heat transfer, perfusion, diffusion, and toxicity constraints are modeled for the declared specimen geometry and parameter ranges.
- Protocol execution records timestamps and measurements needed to demonstrate stabilization within the drift budget.
- Replicate specimens meet preregistered uniformity and reproducibility thresholds without relying on environment-specific behavior.
