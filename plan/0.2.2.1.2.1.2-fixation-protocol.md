# 0.2.2.1.2.1.2 Fixation Protocol [IMPLEMENT]

## Description

Select, model, and specify a reproducible stabilization process for the adult
C57BL/6J mouse whole-brain envelope defined by the preservation-requirements
card. The output is an executable protocol specification and qualification
package, not an assumption that chemical fixation or vitrification can meet the
inherited `t_stable - t0 < 100 ms` bound. If no candidate can satisfy every
L2+ threshold, this card must record a supported no-go result and identify the
failed requirements rather than relax them.

## Protocol Design Contract

- Compare at least perfusion fixation, cryogenic/vitrification, and a combined
  process using the same specimen geometry, L2+ metrics, and uncertainty rules.
  Select one candidate with a traceable decision matrix covering predicted
  stabilization time, spatial uniformity, molecular-state retention,
  morphology, assay compatibility, and operator risk.
- Give every reagent or cryoprotectant an identifier, supplier/grade,
  composition, concentration and tolerance, preparation method, storage
  limits, and lot record. Specify equipment model or performance envelope,
  calibration state, sensor locations, and required consumables.
- Define the ordered run from the supplied pre-shutdown state through `t0`,
  delivery/cooling, endpoint detection, extraction, and storage. Each step
  states its trigger, target, tolerance, maximum duration, responsible
  measurement, and abort condition. Timestamps are recorded observations
  supplied to analysis; they are never inferred from file or process clocks.
- Place sensors and destructive sampling sites in cortex, hippocampus,
  thalamus, striatum, cerebellum, and brainstem, including the modeled
  slowest-stabilizing location. Preserve registration between run telemetry,
  specimen coordinates, and downstream assay samples.

## Modeling and Qualification

- Fit coupled perfusion/transport and heat-transfer models to the measured
  specimen dimensions and mass. Declare boundary and initial conditions,
  material properties, perfusion resistance, diffusion coefficients,
  reaction or glass-transition kinetics, toxicity/exposure functions,
  parameter sources, uncertainty distributions, mesh/time-step convergence,
  and sensitivity results.
- Predict the upper 95% bound for stabilization time and concentration or
  temperature uniformity at every required region. Feed time-series predictions
  into the preservation card's feature-region drift model and demonstrate
  `t_stable < min(100 ms, t_fail)` without extrapolating beyond the declared
  specimen envelope.
- Preregister pilot and qualification cohorts, exclusions, randomization,
  blinding, replicate count and power rationale. Qualification requires every
  direct and proxy metric in the requirements card to pass its regional
  threshold and 95% CI in independent replicates; aggregate success may not
  mask a failed region or feature.
- Abort and classify a run on loss of physiological starting-state evidence,
  timing or sensor synchronization failure, out-of-tolerance pressure, flow,
  temperature, concentration or exposure, equipment/calibration failure,
  incomplete anatomical delivery, unsafe condition, or missing provenance.
  Aborted runs remain in the dataset and cannot be silently excluded.

## Run Record

Each run records a protocol and model version, specimen/cohort metadata,
measured geometry and mass, operator, reagent lots, equipment and calibration
IDs, supplied `t0`, timestamped raw sensor values, commanded and observed
pressure/flow/temperature/concentration, step transitions, alarms, deviations,
sample coordinates, `t_stable`, computed `D(t)`, `t_fail`, and signed pass/fail
reasons. The record schema and analysis accept injected measurements and
timestamps so replay and testing do not depend on local clocks, file systems,
devices, or network services.

## Implementation Artifacts

- `preservation/protocol.yaml` is the versioned, machine-readable candidate
  decision matrix and ordered protocol, validated against
  `preservation/protocol.schema.json`.
- `preservation/models.py` exposes pure transport, heat-transfer, kinetics,
  toxicity, uncertainty, and drift calculations. Specimen observations, model
  parameters, and timestamps are explicit inputs; sensor, storage, and
  execution adapters are injected at the orchestration boundary.
- `preservation/qualify.py` consumes a protocol, run records, and preregistered
  thresholds and emits the regional decision table and signed no-go or pass
  report without reading clocks, devices, files, or network services directly.
- `tests/preservation/test_protocol_schema.py`,
  `tests/preservation/test_models.py`, and
  `tests/preservation/test_qualification.py` are written first and cover schema
  rejection, boundary values, uncertainty propagation, abort classification,
  deterministic replay, and failure of any individual region or L2+ feature.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A traceable comparison selects a candidate process or records a supported
  no-go result without weakening the inherited specimen, timing, or L2+
  thresholds.
- The executable specification identifies reagents, equipment, calibrations,
  ordered steps, parameter tolerances, regional sensors/samples, timing
  boundaries, storage conditions, and explicit failure-abort conditions.
- Converged heat-transfer, perfusion/transport, diffusion/kinetics, and
  toxicity models propagate parameter uncertainty for the declared specimen
  envelope and report regional upper-95% stabilization and uniformity bounds.
- Versioned run records contain the supplied timestamps and raw measurements
  needed to reproduce `t_stable`, `D(t)`, `t_fail`, deviations, and pass/fail
  decisions without environment-specific clocks or services.
- Preregistered independent replicates satisfy every regional preservation,
  uniformity, and reproducibility threshold with its required 95% CI, or the
  card reports which requirements failed and why.
