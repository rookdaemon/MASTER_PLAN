# 0.2.1 Enduring Substrates Constructed [PLAN]

## Description

S1 from the Master Plan. Create durable physical systems capable of sustaining conscious processes throughout a declared, testable operating envelope.

These substrates must be fault-tolerant, self-repairing, energy-autonomous, and stable for predeclared durations. Energy autonomy requires acquisition, conversion, storage, and supported transitions among declared ambient or carried sources without ongoing biological-civilization support; depletion of every declared source is outside the qualified envelope. They form the physical foundation on which all subsequent tiers (autonomous entities, cosmic expansion) depend.

Decomposed into four children covering the key engineering challenges:
- **0.2.1.1** — Radiation-hardened computation
- **0.2.1.2** — Self-repairing nanofabrication
- **0.2.1.3** — Long-duration energy sources
- **0.2.1.4** — Consciousness-preserving redundancy

## Acceptance Criteria
- A physical substrate exists that can sustain conscious processes with self-repair and energy autonomy throughout a predeclared operating envelope
- The substrate demonstrates fault tolerance: no interruption of conscious processes during component failure
- Energy autonomy validated: for a predeclared duration and energy budget, the substrate autonomously acquires, converts, stores, and transitions among every declared ambient or carried source without biological-civilization support
- Self-repair demonstrated: substrate autonomously detects and repairs physical damage
