# 0.1.2.2.5 Publication and Replication Completed [PLAN]
## Description

Publish the predictive model and verification results through peer review and obtain independent replications of the core biological and non-biological findings.

## File Manifest
- docs/consciousness-science/predictive-consciousness-models.md

## Acceptance Criteria
- At least one peer-reviewed publication has a durable publication identifier and links the model, methods, data, and analyses
- At least two research groups independent of the originating group complete preregistered replications
- Replication records identify institutions, investigators, predictions tested, datasets, analyses, and outcomes
- The synthesis distinguishes successful, partial, and failed replications and updates the model's supported scope accordingly
