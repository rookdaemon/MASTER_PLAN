# 0.6.4 Heat-Death Mitigation Research [PLAN]

## Description

Define a risk-tiered research program for sustaining subjective experience as usable free-energy gradients disappear. Evaluate reversible computation, vacuum-energy extraction, false-vacuum transitions, and baby-universe proposals with explicit energy models, evidence-readiness gates, catastrophic-risk boundaries, and experience-preservation constraints.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `docs/cosmological-longevity/heat-death-mitigation-research.md` gives dimensionally consistent energy, temperature, computation-rate, and duration models for reversible computation and every speculative framework assigned a quantitative claim
- Each framework has declared physical assumptions, evidence readiness, uncertainty, falsification conditions, research priority, and a rule preventing speculative energy from entering the baseline longevity budget
- The program defines promotion, pause, and abandonment gates that can be evaluated from recorded evidence without discretionary reinterpretation
- False-vacuum and universe-transition work remains non-activating unless independent safety evidence satisfies the ethical and catastrophic-failure controls; monitoring literature alone cannot authorize an experiment
- Every viable path states how subjective temporal resolution, coherence, checkpoints, welfare limits, and continuity are preserved, and labels survival of information alone as insufficient
