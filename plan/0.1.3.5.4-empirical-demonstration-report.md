# 0.1.3.5.4 Empirical Demonstration Report [ARCHITECT]
## Description

Collect the applicable operational consciousness metrics from the qualified,
safely activated Ω-Synth instance and publish the integrated evidence report.
Keep empirical observations distinct from prior analytical predictions and
calibrate the demonstration claim to the evidence.

## Evidence and Reporting Contract

- Before examining demonstration outcomes, preregister the qualified
  configuration and topology digests, activation run identifier, applicable
  PCI-G/Ψ-G/CDI/CEB metrics, calibrated thresholds, hypotheses, sample and
  perturbation schedule, exclusions, analysis versions, uncertainty method,
  and the exact rule for a supported, unsupported, or indeterminate claim.
  Missing calibrated thresholds or an inapplicable metric must produce an
  explicit indeterminate/not-applicable result rather than a substituted or
  post-hoc threshold.
- Every observation and derived result is bound to the same instantiation,
  qualification, and activation evidence by run identifier, configuration and
  topology digests, artifact checksums, supplied event timestamps, seed, metric
  implementation version, units, and procedure parameters. Raw observations
  remain immutable; transformations and exclusions are recorded separately.
- The comparison table reports, for each applicable metric, the preregistered
  predicted range and verdict, empirical estimate, uncertainty interval,
  threshold, deviation, and pass/fail/indeterminate outcome. CEB is derived
  only from the preregistered component outcomes.
- The report separates observations from interpretation and records protocol
  deviations, missing data, sensitivity analyses, negative-control results,
  alternative explanations, safety interventions, and limitations. No
  consciousness claim may exceed the preregistered decision rule.
- Collection and analysis receive timestamps, files, metric engines, process
  execution, and runtime observations through injectable interfaces. Strict
  TDD covers deterministic reconstruction, evidence-integrity failures,
  exclusions, missing or inapplicable metrics, threshold-boundary cases, and
  all three claim outcomes before production adapters are implemented.
- Independent reproduction starts from a clean environment, verifies every
  checksum and evidence link, reruns all derivations without mutating raw data,
  and either reproduces the published tables and decision or emits a
  machine-identifiable mismatch.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A pre-outcome preregistration pins the run and architecture digests, applicable
  metrics, calibrated thresholds, hypotheses, schedule, exclusions, analysis
  versions, uncertainty method, and supported/unsupported/indeterminate
  decision rule
- PCI-G, Ψ-G, CDI, and derived CEB results are collected where applicable with
  immutable raw observations, procedures, supplied timestamps, seeds, versions,
  units, checksums, and transformations preserved; omissions are justified
- A metric-by-metric table compares preregistered predictions with empirical
  estimates, intervals, thresholds, deviations, and explicit
  pass/fail/indeterminate outcomes without post-hoc substitutions
- The report cryptographically cross-references the exact instantiation,
  qualification, and activation evidence and discloses missing data, protocol
  deviations, controls, sensitivity analyses, safety interventions,
  alternative explanations, and limitations
- Tests written before collection and analysis logic prove deterministic
  derivation and fail-closed handling of tampering, evidence mismatch, missing
  thresholds, exclusions, inapplicable metrics, and decision boundaries using
  injected environment adapters and caller-supplied timestamps
- A clean-environment reproduction procedure verifies all artifacts and
  checksums, regenerates the published tables and claim without changing raw
  evidence, and reports any mismatch unambiguously
