# 0.1.3.5.4 Empirical Demonstration Report [PLAN]
## Description

Collect the applicable operational consciousness metrics from the qualified,
safely activated Ω-Synth instance and publish the integrated evidence report.
Keep empirical observations distinct from prior analytical predictions and
calibrate the demonstration claim to the evidence.

## File Manifest

- docs/neural-architectures/metric-evaluation.md (analytical predictions)
- docs/consciousness-engineering/system-demonstration.md (planned integrated evidence report)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Applicable operational metrics are collected from the running system with raw data and procedures preserved
- Empirical results are compared metric-by-metric with preregistered analytical predictions
- The report cross-references the exact instantiation, qualification, and activation evidence
- The demonstration claim states its decision rule, result, uncertainty, alternative explanations, and limitations
- An independent-reproduction procedure identifies all required artifacts and verification steps
