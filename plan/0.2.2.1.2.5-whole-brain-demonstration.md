# 0.2.2.1.2.5 Whole-Brain Demonstration [PLAN]

## Description

Integrate preservation, acquisition, reconstruction, and serialization in one traceable biological whole-brain scan and qualify the result for simulation initialization.

## Acceptance Criteria

- One complete biological brain at a declared species and specimen scale passes the preservation, acquisition, reconstruction, and schema contracts.
- Coverage, missing-data rate, accuracy, registration, timing, and integrity results are reported from measured evidence rather than synthetic fixtures.
- Independent ground-truth assays meet all registered thresholds, with failures and uncertainty retained in the report.
- The stored dataset is loaded successfully by the 0.2.2.1.3 simulation initialization interface.
