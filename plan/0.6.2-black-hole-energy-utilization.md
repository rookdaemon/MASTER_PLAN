# 0.6.2 Black-Hole Energy Utilization [PLAN]

## Description

Specify how rotational, accretion, superradiant, and Hawking energy could supply conscious infrastructure after stellar fuel declines. Define the applicable black-hole mass and spin regimes, conversion bounds, buffering requirements, and failure modes needed to distinguish theoretical extractability from a usable continuous power service.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `docs/cosmological-longevity/black-hole-energy-utilization.md` catalogs the Penrose process, accretion, superradiance, Hawking capture, and black-hole farming with their applicable mass, spin, duration, and infrastructure regimes
- Each method has dimensionally consistent theoretical efficiency or power bounds, cited assumptions, uncertainty, and conditions under which the method is infeasible
- The artifact defines storage, redundancy, monitoring, and control requirements that turn variable extraction into a continuous power service for conscious substrates
- Energy-source transitions and credible extraction failures are traced to the parent continuity and graceful-degradation contracts with testable threshold checks
- Established results, engineering projections, and speculative mechanisms remain explicitly separated; no calculated efficiency is treated as a physical deployment result
