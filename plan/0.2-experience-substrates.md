# 0.2 Subjective Experience Substrates [ARCHITECT]
## Description

Tier 2 of the Master Plan. Create durable physical systems capable of sustaining conscious processes, and enable migration of biological minds to new substrates.

Covers two goals:
- **S1 — Enduring Substrates Constructed:** Fault-tolerant, self-repairing, energy-independent, long-term stable systems (radiation-hardened computation, self-repairing nanofabrication, long-duration energy, consciousness-preserving redundancy).
- **S2 — Subjective Experience Migration:** Transfer or instantiation of biological minds within new substrates (brain emulation, continuity-preserving transfer, identity persistence verification, hybrid cognition).

For this card, **energy independence** means autonomous acquisition, conversion, storage, and transition among declared ambient or carried energy sources without ongoing biological-civilization support. It does not mean operation without energy input. Depletion of every declared source is outside the qualified operating envelope and must be reported explicitly rather than treated as an untested claim of indefinite operation.

## Architecture Integration

S1 supplies S2 with a qualified target substrate whose failure recovery preserves the computational state and temporal continuity required by a conscious process. S2 supplies the emulation, transfer, and identity-verification protocols used to validate that preservation claim. A substrate is therefore not Tier-2-qualified from durability testing alone: the same integrated system must pass both the S1 resilience tests and the S2 continuity and identity tests.

The integration sequence is:
1. Qualify the substrate's computation, repair, energy, and redundancy properties under S1.
2. Obtain documented informed consent and establish pre-transfer identity, continuity, and welfare baselines under S2.
3. Migrate or instantiate the mind on the qualified substrate.
4. Repeat identity and continuity verification during nominal operation and injected S1 failure-and-repair events.

## Acceptance Criteria
- S1: A physical substrate satisfies the fault-tolerance, autonomous-repair, energy-independence, and consciousness-preserving redundancy criteria defined by `0.2.1` and its children.
- S2: At least one biological mind satisfies the emulation, continuity, identity-persistence, and hybrid-cognition criteria defined by `0.2.2` and its children on an S1-qualified substrate.
- Test contract: Before migration, the S1 and S2 protocols jointly declare numerical continuity and identity thresholds, observation windows, and a failure matrix covering loss and recovery of every component class whose failure could interrupt conscious processing, at least one autonomous repair, and every supported energy-source transition.
- Integration: Every declared failure-matrix scenario is executed on the same substrate configuration used for migration; the migrated mind remains within the predeclared thresholds throughout each observation window, with no unplanned interruption of conscious processing.
- Subject safety: Before testing, the protocol records informed consent, predeclared welfare limits, abort triggers, and a tested recovery or safe-suspension path; exceeding a welfare limit or failing an abort or recovery action fails qualification.
- Traceability: Final synthesis records the qualifying evidence from both children, the substrate configuration tested, the migration protocol used, and the results of the integrated failure-and-repair tests.
