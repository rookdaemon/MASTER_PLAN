# 0.1.2.2.1.2.1.1 Calibration Protocol Specified [ARCHITECT]
## Description

Write the human-readable calibration protocol before outcome-guided fitting. Freeze the population, sampling frame, outcome adjudication, inclusion and exclusion rules, missing-data handling, preprocessing, fitting objective and constraints, inference, minimum sample or precision requirements, sensitivity and substrate-stratified analyses, and deviation reporting. Every scientific or statistical decision must have a computable formula or executable procedure.

## File Manifest
- docs/consciousness-science/irsm-calibration-protocol.md

## Acceptance Criteria
- The protocol unambiguously specifies the population, sampling, outcomes, adjudication, exclusions, missing-data handling, and preprocessing required by the parent calibration-study card
- The fitting objective, normalization bounds, parameter constraints, optimizer inputs, deterministic tie-breaking, convergence and failure rules are fixed without inspecting held-out outcomes
- Discrimination and calibration estimands, confidence intervals, multiplicity policy, minimum sample or precision requirements, sensitivity analyses, and substrate-stratified reporting are computable
- Exclusions and deviations are logged, and both preregistered and deviation-aware results are required without silently replacing the primary analysis
- The protocol defines the immutable identifiers and reciprocal cross-references that the dataset, partition, executable analysis, and preregistration record must contain
