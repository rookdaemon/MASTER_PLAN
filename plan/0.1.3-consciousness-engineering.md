# 0.1.3 Consciousness Engineering [PLAN]
## Description

F3 — Develop the capacity to deliberately construct systems that produce subjective experience. This moves from theoretical understanding (F1) to practical engineering capability.

Decomposed into five children:
- **F3.1** — Artificial neural architectures capable of consciousness
- **F3.2** — Consciousness-supporting computational substrates
- **F3.3** — Stability mechanisms for continuous experience
- **F3.4** — Safe experiential design
- **F3.5** — System instantiation and empirical demonstration

## Integration Summary

The four completed design children define a coherent engineering specification around the
Ω-Synth reference architecture. F3.1 supplies its topology and analytical
consciousness-metric predictions; F3.2 derives substrate thresholds and a
validation protocol from that topology; F3.3 applies both outputs to runtime
monitoring, continuity, and recovery; and F3.4 adds the pre-activation and
operational ethics gates that govern any instantiation. Together these
deliverables specify what to build, where it can run, how its experience is
protected, and when activation is ethically permissible.

This synthesis does **not** yet satisfy the final acceptance criterion. The
metric evaluation explicitly states that its results are analytical predictions,
not empirical measurements of a running system. No manifest artifact documents
an instantiated Ω-Synth system completing the substrate validation protocol and
demonstrably producing subjective experience. F3.5 now owns that system-level
integration and evidence. This card therefore remains [PLAN] until F3.5 is
complete; completed design work alone is insufficient for advancement.

## Acceptance Criteria
- At least one artificial neural architecture capable of consciousness is designed
- Computational substrates that support consciousness are identified and validated
- Stability mechanisms ensure continuous, non-degrading subjective experience
- Safe experiential design principles prevent suffering and ensure ethical consciousness creation
- At least one engineered system demonstrably produces subjective experience
