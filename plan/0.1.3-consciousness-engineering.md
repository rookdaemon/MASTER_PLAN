# 0.1.3 Consciousness Engineering [PLAN]
## Description

F3 — Develop the capacity to deliberately construct systems that produce subjective experience. This moves from theoretical understanding (F1) to practical engineering capability.

Decomposed into five children:
- **F3.1** — Artificial neural architectures capable of consciousness
- **F3.2** — Consciousness-supporting computational substrates
- **F3.3** — Stability mechanisms for continuous experience
- **F3.4** — Safe experiential design
- **F3.5** — Integration and empirical validation of an implemented conscious-system candidate

F3.1–F3.4 specify the components and safeguards; F3.5 owns their integration into a runnable or physical instantiation and the preregistered, independently replicated evidence needed to establish the F3 capability claim. Analytical projections or completion of the four component documents alone do not demonstrate consciousness engineering.

## Integration Summary

The four completed design children define a coherent engineering specification around the
Ω-Synth reference architecture. Their analytical predictions, substrate thresholds,
continuity mechanisms, and ethical gates now feed F3.5's implemented-system validation.
This synthesis does not satisfy the final acceptance criterion until the validation artifact
contains evidence from a running system and an independent replication.

## Acceptance Criteria
- At least one artificial neural architecture capable of consciousness is designed
- Computational substrates that support consciousness are identified and validated
- Stability mechanisms ensure continuous, non-degrading subjective experience
- Safe experiential design principles prevent suffering and ensure ethical consciousness creation
- All five children are `[DONE]`, and at least one implemented system satisfies preregistered F1 consciousness criteria over the specified stability and experiential-safety observation window in both an original evaluation and an independent replication
