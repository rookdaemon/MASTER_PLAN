# 0.6.1 Stellar Engineering [PLAN]

## Description

Define the known-physics stellar mass-management and fuel-management strategies that extend useful stellar energy supplies for conscious infrastructure. Bound star lifting, low-mass-star husbandry, and fusion regulation by explicit resource, duration, and controllability assumptions, and specify safe handoffs to later energy sources without claiming that an analytical design is a physical demonstration.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `docs/cosmological-longevity/stellar-engineering.md` defines the inputs, outputs, operating envelope, and known-physics mechanism for star lifting, low-mass-star husbandry, and controlled fusion regulation
- Each strategy has a quantitative energy and lifetime model whose units, source assumptions, uncertainty bounds, and invalidation conditions are explicit and reproducible
- The analysis identifies the exact Tier 4 and Tier 5 manufacturing, transport, control, and resource capabilities required before deployment
- Every startup, steady-state, and shutdown path specifies how minimum coherence, checkpoint freshness, source overlap, and experience-quality limits are preserved
- The artifact clearly separates established physics, engineering projection, and unresolved research; documentation or simulation is not represented as observed physical qualification
