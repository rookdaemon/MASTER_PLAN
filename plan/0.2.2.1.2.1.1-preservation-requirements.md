# 0.2.2.1.2.1.1 Preservation Requirements [DONE]

## Description

Translate the L2+ fidelity contract into measurable preservation targets for an adult laboratory-mouse whole brain. This card defines requirements and validation inputs; 0.2.2.1.2.1.2 selects the fixation process and 0.2.2.1.2.1.3 validates it.

## Specimen and Timing Contract

- **Species/cohort:** adult C57BL/6J mouse, either sex, 8–16 weeks; sex, age, body mass, and deviations are stratification metadata.
- **Whole-brain envelope:** intact encephalon, nominally 10 × 8 × 6 mm and 0.35–0.55 g. Every protocol run records measured dimensions and mass; specimens outside this envelope fail this target rather than being extrapolated.
- **Starting state:** deeply anesthetized, normothermic (36–38 °C), oxygenated, perfused animal with stable cardiorespiratory observations immediately before physiological shutdown. Shutdown time `t0` and all observations are supplied as recorded inputs, not inferred from execution time.
- **Completed stabilization:** `t_stable` is the earliest supplied timestamp at which every sampled brain region meets the protocol's declared temperature or chemical-arrest endpoint and remains within it.
- **Maximum interval:** `t_stable - t0 < 100 ms`. This is an L2+ requirement inherited from the whole-brain-scanning drift budget, not a claim that an existing whole-brain method achieves it. A protocol that cannot demonstrate this bound fails.

## Preservation Requirements

All errors are measured against matched unfixed controls or pre-shutdown measurements, with region-stratified sampling covering cortex, hippocampus, thalamus, striatum, cerebellum, and brainstem. Report the point estimate and two-sided 95% confidence interval (CI); the CI must remain within the threshold.

| L2+ state | Direct or proxy | Metric and sampling method | Pass threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuron and glial morphology/topology | Direct | Blinded isotropic EM stereology in systematic-random blocks: membrane continuity, neurite diameter, spine density, astrocyte-domain overlap, and gap-junction density | Each regional normalized absolute error ≤5%; membrane discontinuities ≤1% of traced profiles |
| Synapse map and type | Direct | Correlative EM on registered blocks, scored against unfixed rapid-freeze controls | Synapse detection sensitivity and specificity ≥0.95; type agreement ≥0.95 |
| Synaptic weight | Validated proxy | Vesicle-pool/active-zone morphology plus receptor-density model calibrated against paired electrophysiology | Normalized RMSE ≤0.15 and regional bias magnitude ≤0.05 |
| Receptor distributions | Direct abundance; state is proxy | Multiplexed quantitative labeling on registered blocks, with spike-in standards | Per-target regional density error ≤10%; Pearson correlation across sampled synapses ≥0.90 |
| Neuromodulatory baseline (DA, 5-HT, ACh, NE) | Direct concentration | Isotope-dilution mass spectrometry on systematic-random regional punches | Absolute concentration error ≤10% for every analyte and region |
| Ion-channel state/noise statistics | Validated proxy | Channel-type abundance and phosphorylation/conformation markers calibrated to pre-shutdown patch-clamp distributions | Distributional Jensen–Shannon divergence ≤0.05 and predicted open-probability RMSE ≤0.10 |
| Pre-shutdown activity snapshot | Direct before `t0`; preserved linkage is proxy | Supplied electrophysiology ending at `t0`, registered to sampled tissue and compared with state reconstructed from preserved measurements | ≥95% sampled channels linked; spike-rate and membrane-state reconstruction normalized RMSE ≤0.10 |

No unlisted surrogate may satisfy a direct measurement. A proxy is admissible only when its calibration dataset, model version, held-out error, applicability domain, and uncertainty propagation are recorded. Missing required state, a CI crossing its threshold, an out-of-domain proxy, or an unsupported feature is a preservation failure.

## Brain-State Drift Model

For each required feature `i` and sampled region `r`, compute normalized drift
`d(i,r,t) = |x(i,r,t) - x(i,r,t0)| / T(i)`, where `T(i)` is that feature's maximum error from the table. For distributional metrics, use the stated divergence divided by its threshold. Propagate measurement and proxy uncertainty by using the upper 95% confidence bound.

The whole-brain drift score is `D(t) = max(i,r) upper95[d(i,r,t)]`. The interval is acceptable only if `D(t) < 1` for every supplied observation through `t_stable`. The protocol card must fit a conservative monotone model for each feature-region series (or use a preregistered mechanistic model), report parameters and goodness of fit, and solve for `t_fail = inf{t ≥ t0 : D(t) ≥ 1}`. It must demonstrate `t_stable < min(100 ms, t_fail)`. Sparse observations that cannot bound `t_fail` with 95% confidence fail rather than justify interpolation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The specimen species, brain dimensions, mass range, physiological starting state, and maximum fixation interval are explicit.
- Every required L2+ feature has a preservation metric, sampling method, threshold, unit, and uncertainty requirement.
- A quantitative brain-state drift model justifies the maximum interval from physiological shutdown to completed stabilization.
- Requirements distinguish directly preserved state from validated proxies and identify unsupported features as failures.
