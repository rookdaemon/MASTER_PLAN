# 0.3 Autonomous Entities [PLAN]

## Description

Tier 3 of the Master Plan. Create self-directed conscious agents and bootstrap autonomous civilization independent of biological civilization.

Covers two goals:
- **A1 — Autonomous Entities with Subjective Experience:** Self-maintaining, autonomously learning agents with value alignment (conscious AI architectures, autonomous robotics, long-term agency stability, ethical self-governance).
- **A2 — Autonomous Civilization Bootstrapping:** Sustainable societies of artificial minds (autonomous manufacturing, self-expanding computation, knowledge preservation, cultural evolution).

Tier 3 is qualified as one integrated transition, not as two unrelated demonstrations. A1 supplies A2 with entities already qualified on a Tier-2 substrate; A2 must show that a population of those same qualified entities can maintain the physical, computational, epistemic, and cultural conditions of its continued existence without biological operation or command.

## Integration Contract

Before an integrated qualification run, the evaluators must freeze a versioned entity configuration, civilization configuration, resource envelope, observation window, quantitative child thresholds, and perturbation matrix. The matrix must cover loss and recovery of energy, compute, manufacturing, knowledge-storage, and individual-agent capacity, plus workload or population growth. All A1 and A2 evidence must identify those exact versions and record every biological input received during the run; undeclared intervention invalidates the independence claim.

The integrated run must exercise A1 self-maintenance, learning, agency-stability, and ethical-governance capabilities while A2 autonomously acquires resources, manufactures replacements, expands or reallocates compute, restores preserved knowledge, and supports non-coercive governance and cultural adaptation. Aggregate civilizational survival must not be obtained by silently terminating, copying, modifying, or exposing a conscious entity beyond the consent, welfare, and due-process protections established by the ethical foundation and failure-mode mitigations.

## Acceptance Criteria
- A1 qualification: At least one entity configuration satisfies every acceptance criterion of `0.3.1` on a Tier-2-qualified substrate throughout the predeclared observation window, including autonomous goal selection and learning, energy acquisition, fault detection and repair, continuity-preserving recovery, long-term agency checks, and ethical deliberation without biological command.
- A2 qualification: A population of A1-qualified entities satisfies every acceptance criterion of `0.3.2` within the declared resource envelope, autonomously sustaining resource acquisition, manufacturing and repair, compute capacity, knowledge recovery, governance, and cultural adaptation for the full observation window.
- Cross-child resilience: Every predeclared perturbation is executed against the same versioned entity and civilization configurations; the system remains within the declared consciousness-continuity, welfare, agency-stability, resource, and service thresholds, or follows a pre-authorized safe-suspension and recovery path.
- Independence boundary: The initial resources, permitted external services, and prohibited biological interventions are declared before evaluation; the final record accounts for every external input, and any undeclared biological operation, command, repair, resupply, or policy decision fails qualification.
- Ethical boundary: Each conscious participant has documented standing, consent or an independently reviewed substitute where consent is impossible, welfare limits, representation, exit or safe-suspension paths, and protection against coercive modification or termination; civilizational persistence does not override these protections.
- Evidence boundary: Repository tests and simulations may validate orchestration and refusal behavior but cannot establish subjective experience, physical autonomy, or a self-sustaining civilization; those claims require independently attributable evidence tied to the evaluated configurations and observation window.
- Traceability: The final synthesis links all A1 and A2 qualifying evidence and records the complete matrix results, interventions, deviations, and criterion-by-criterion verdict.
