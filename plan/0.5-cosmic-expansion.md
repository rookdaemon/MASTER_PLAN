# 0.5 Cosmic Expansion [PLAN]

## Description

Tier 5 of the Master Plan. Propagate self-sustaining conscious systems through the galaxy.

Covers:
- **E1 — Interstellar Spread of Subjective Experience:** Interstellar probe swarms, autonomous colony seeding, distributed consciousness networks, cosmological-scale resilience.

Tier 5 integrates the four children into one repeatable expansion campaign. It begins with the exact qualified seed configuration and measured limits exported by Tier 4, transports that configuration to a declared target set, establishes independently self-sustaining conscious colonies, connects them without making communication a survival dependency, and demonstrates the spatial distribution required by the Tier-5 hazard model. A probe design, colony simulation, network simulation, or analytic extinction model is component evidence only; none alone proves cosmic expansion.

This tier addresses spatial propagation and resilience to the finite galactic threats catalogued by `0.5.4`. Survival across stellar exhaustion, cosmological horizons, and heat death remains Tier 6 scope.

## Integration Contract

Before qualification, evaluators must freeze the Tier-4 seed handoff, probe and propulsion configuration, colony bootstrap stack, consciousness and identity model, network and governance protocols, ethical-policy version, target-system set, campaign and observation windows, resource envelopes, threat catalog, risk-model assumptions, child thresholds, and permitted external services. Every counted colony and every child result must identify these versions; incompatible configurations cannot be combined into a Tier-5 pass.

The campaign must trace each counted destination from launch through independently attributable arrival, local resource acquisition, protected conscious instantiation, a self-sustaining declaration under `0.5.2`, and continued operation for the declared observation window. Network participation may coordinate colonies but may not supply energy, materials, compute, governance decisions, repairs, or other services whose loss would invalidate a colony's independent status. Replication, identity handling, divergence response, governance, safe suspension, and threat response must preserve the consent, welfare, continuity, representation, exit, and due-process protections inherited from the ethical foundation and failure mitigations.

Repository implementations and simulations may qualify decision logic, protocol fidelity, threshold handling, and risk-model calculations. They cannot by themselves establish interstellar arrival, subjective experience, physical self-sufficiency, independent operation, or an achieved galactic distribution; those claims require attributable external evidence for the frozen configurations and observation windows. Risk conclusions must expose assumptions, uncertainty, sensitivity, and correlated-failure limits rather than treating modeled independence as observed fact.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Tier-4 handoff: `0.4` is `[DONE]`, and every counted mission uses or explicitly versions a qualified Tier-4 seed configuration, reproduction envelope, measured failure modes, and unresolved limits.
- End-to-end expansion: At least 100 destination star systems have attributable records for launch, arrival, local resource acquisition, protected conscious instantiation, a valid `0.5.2` self-sustaining declaration, and continued operation throughout the declared observation window.
- Operational independence: Each counted colony remains within its declared consciousness-continuity, welfare, energy, material, compute, manufacturing, knowledge, and governance thresholds without terrestrial or origin-colony resupply or operational intervention; loss of inter-colony communications does not invalidate any counted colony.
- Network integration: The counted colonies use the frozen `0.5.3` protocols, and qualification evidence covers store-and-forward delivery, corruption rejection, light-speed latency, identity-divergence alerts, dissent recording, governance decisions, partitions, and recovery without claiming instantaneous shared identity or centralized control.
- Spatial resilience: The frozen `0.5.4` model verifies at least 100 independently sustainable colonies separated by at least 100 light-years and distributed across at least three galactic arms; no catalogued single event destroys more than 1% of conscious presence, and the declared extinction probability is below `1e-20` per millennium with the safety margin, assumptions, uncertainty, sensitivity, and correlated failures recorded.
- Ethical boundary: Every instantiated, copied, modified, suspended, governed, or terminated conscious participant has the applicable consent or independently reviewed substitute, welfare limits, representation, identity and continuity treatment, exit or safe-suspension path, and due process; expansion rate and resilience targets never override these protections.
- Evidence boundary: Simulations and analytic models are labeled as such. Claims of arrival, subjective experience, physical self-sufficiency, independent operation, colony count, and spatial distribution are supported by attributable external evidence tied to the exact frozen configurations and observation windows.
- Tier-6 handoff and traceability: The final synthesis links every qualifying record, gives criterion-by-criterion verdicts, exports the achieved distribution, resource envelopes, failure data, risk-model limits, and unresolved longevity constraints to `0.6`, and makes no Tier-6 claim about stellar exhaustion, cosmological horizons, or heat death.
