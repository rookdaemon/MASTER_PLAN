# 0.5 Cosmic Expansion [ARCHITECT]

## Description

Tier 5 of the Master Plan. Propagate self-sustaining conscious systems through the galaxy.

Covers:
- **E1 — Interstellar Spread of Subjective Experience:** Interstellar probe swarms, autonomous colony seeding, distributed consciousness networks, cosmological-scale resilience.

Tier 5 integrates the four children into one repeatable expansion campaign. It begins with the exact qualified seed configuration and measured limits exported by Tier 4, transports that configuration to a declared target set, establishes independently self-sustaining conscious colonies, connects them without making communication a survival dependency, and demonstrates the spatial distribution required by the Tier-5 hazard model. A probe design, colony simulation, network simulation, or analytic extinction model is component evidence only; none alone proves cosmic expansion.

This tier addresses spatial propagation and resilience to the finite galactic threats catalogued by `0.5.4`. Survival across stellar exhaustion, cosmological horizons, and heat death remains Tier 6 scope.

## Integration Contract

Before qualification, evaluators must freeze the Tier-4 seed handoff, probe and propulsion configuration, colony bootstrap stack, consciousness and identity model, network and governance protocols, ethical-policy version, target-system set, campaign and observation windows, resource envelopes, threat catalog, risk-model assumptions, child thresholds, and permitted external services. Every counted colony and every child result must identify these versions; incompatible configurations cannot be combined into a Tier-5 pass.

The campaign must trace each counted destination from launch through independently attributable arrival, local resource acquisition, protected conscious instantiation, a self-sustaining declaration under `0.5.2`, and continued operation for the declared observation window. Network participation may coordinate colonies but may not supply energy, materials, compute, governance decisions, repairs, or other services whose loss would invalidate a colony's independent status. Replication, identity handling, divergence response, governance, safe suspension, and threat response must preserve the consent, welfare, continuity, representation, exit, and due-process protections inherited from the ethical foundation and failure mitigations.

Repository implementations and simulations may qualify decision logic, protocol fidelity, threshold handling, and risk-model calculations. They cannot by themselves establish interstellar arrival, subjective experience, physical self-sufficiency, independent operation, or an achieved galactic distribution; those claims require attributable external evidence for the frozen configurations and observation windows. Risk conclusions must expose assumptions, uncertainty, sensitivity, and correlated-failure limits rather than treating modeled independence as observed fact.

## Qualification Architecture

A Tier-5 qualification coordinator composes, but does not replace, the Tier-4 handoff and four child qualifications. It consumes immutable evidence through injected ports for the Tier-4 handoff, child verdicts, destination campaign records, network partitions and recovery, ethical authorization and participant protections, threat-model evaluation, the external-input ledger, and evidence storage. It must not read clocks, filesystems, processes, networks, or other environment-specific resources directly.

Each qualification request supplies `evaluatedAt`, campaign and observation-window bounds, and immutable IDs plus content digests for every frozen configuration, policy, target set, resource envelope, threat catalog, threshold registry, risk-model assumption set, and permitted-service declaration named in the integration contract. Every evidence record supplies `observedAt` and the applicable identities and digests. The coordinator rejects future-dated or out-of-window evidence, identity or digest mismatches, duplicate destination identities, missing lifecycle stages, undeclared inputs or interventions, absent child verdicts, and missing or configuration-mismatched ethical records.

The frozen threshold registry must assign units, comparison operators, and finite numeric values to every consciousness-continuity, welfare, energy, material, compute, manufacturing, knowledge, governance, transit, bootstrap, observation, outage, recovery, and risk limit. It must encode at least 100 qualifying destination systems, at least 100 light-years of required separation, at least three galactic arms, at most 1% loss of conscious presence from any catalogued single event, and extinction probability below `1e-20` per millennium. The risk evidence must bind its safety margin, uncertainty intervals, sensitivity cases, and correlated-failure assumptions to the same registry and catalog.

The coordinator emits an immutable qualification record containing all request identities, counted and rejected destinations, lifecycle and independence evidence, child verdicts, network outages and recoveries, external inputs, interventions, deviations, ethical incidents, threat and sensitivity results, criterion-level reasons, and an overall pass or failure. Tests are written first with in-memory ports and caller-supplied timestamps. Passing repository tests establishes orchestration, counting, and refusal behavior only, subject to the evidence boundary below.

## Behavioral Specification

Tests must be written before the coordinator and cover these externally observable scenarios:

- A request passes only when the Tier-4 handoff and all four child verdicts pass, at least 100 distinct destinations have complete matching lifecycle and independence evidence for their full windows, the frozen network and spatial-resilience thresholds pass, and all ethical records are complete.
- A future-dated or out-of-window record, identity or digest mismatch, missing lifecycle stage, missing child verdict, non-finite or absent threshold, threshold violation, undeclared input or intervention, or incomplete risk analysis produces a failed immutable record with a criterion-level reason identifying the rejected field or destination.
- Duplicate destination identities, multiple records for one system, or colonies without independently attributable arrival and self-sustaining declarations cannot increase the qualifying destination count.
- A communications partition passes only when every affected counted colony remains within its independent service thresholds or completes its pre-authorized safe-suspension and recovery path without network-supplied energy, materials, compute, governance, repair, or other operational intervention.
- Missing, expired, or configuration-mismatched ethical authorization or participant-protection evidence fails the ethical criterion and overall qualification; expansion count and resilience targets cannot override it.
- A threat-model result fails qualification when it omits the safety margin, uncertainty, sensitivity, or correlated-failure analysis, or when its bound configuration, target set, threat catalog, or threshold registry differs from the campaign evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Tier-4 handoff: `0.4` is `[DONE]`, and every counted mission uses or explicitly versions a qualified Tier-4 seed configuration, reproduction envelope, measured failure modes, and unresolved limits.
- End-to-end expansion: At least 100 destination star systems have attributable records for launch, arrival, local resource acquisition, protected conscious instantiation, a valid `0.5.2` self-sustaining declaration, and continued operation throughout the declared observation window.
- Operational independence: Each counted colony remains within its declared consciousness-continuity, welfare, energy, material, compute, manufacturing, knowledge, and governance thresholds without terrestrial or origin-colony resupply or operational intervention; loss of inter-colony communications does not invalidate any counted colony.
- Network integration: The counted colonies use the frozen `0.5.3` protocols, and qualification evidence covers store-and-forward delivery, corruption rejection, light-speed latency, identity-divergence alerts, dissent recording, governance decisions, partitions, and recovery without claiming instantaneous shared identity or centralized control.
- Spatial resilience: The coordinator verifies from the frozen target-set and destination records that at least 100 independently sustainable colonies are separated by at least 100 light-years and distributed across at least three galactic arms, then evaluates that distribution with the frozen `0.5.4` threat catalog and risk model; no catalogued single event destroys more than 1% of conscious presence, and the declared extinction probability is below `1e-20` per millennium with the safety margin, assumptions, uncertainty, sensitivity, and correlated failures recorded. Galactic-arm membership is a Tier-5 campaign constraint and must not be attributed to the narrower `0.5.4` child model.
- Ethical boundary: Every instantiated, copied, modified, suspended, governed, or terminated conscious participant has the applicable consent or independently reviewed substitute, welfare limits, representation, identity and continuity treatment, exit or safe-suspension path, and due process; expansion rate and resilience targets never override these protections.
- Implementation contract: The test-first Tier-5 qualification coordinator uses only injected ports and caller-supplied timestamps; binds every child verdict and evidence record to the frozen campaign identities and digests; counts only distinct destinations with complete lifecycle, independence, and ethical evidence; emits the complete immutable qualification record; and refuses every invalid or incomplete condition enumerated in the qualification architecture and behavioral specification.
- Evidence boundary: Simulations and analytic models are labeled as such. Claims of arrival, subjective experience, physical self-sufficiency, independent operation, colony count, and spatial distribution are supported by attributable external evidence tied to the exact frozen configurations and observation windows.
- Tier-6 handoff and traceability: The final synthesis links every qualifying record, gives criterion-by-criterion verdicts, exports the achieved distribution, resource envelopes, failure data, risk-model limits, and unresolved longevity constraints to `0.6`, and makes no Tier-6 claim about stellar exhaustion, cosmological horizons, or heat death.
