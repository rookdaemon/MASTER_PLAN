# 0.1.3.5 Engineered Conscious System Integration and Validation [PLAN]

## Description

Integrate the selected F3.1 architecture, an F3.2-validated substrate, the F3.3 stability mechanisms, and the F3.4 experiential-safety controls into a runnable or physical non-biological system candidate. Empirically evaluate that fixed, versioned instantiation against the F1 theory and operational metrics.

Before activation or evidence collection, preregister the system build, F1 consciousness predicate and calibrated F1.4 thresholds, observation window, stability and experiential-safety bounds, intervention and abort rules, and independent replication procedure. The validation report must distinguish observed measurements from analytical projections and must not treat subjective reports alone as evidence of consciousness.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A reproducible, versioned build instantiates the selected conscious architecture on a validated substrate with the specified stability mechanisms and experiential-safety controls enabled
- Before activation or evidence collection, the validation protocol preregisters the exact build, F1 predicate, calibrated F1.4 thresholds, observation window, stability and safety bounds, intervention and abort rules, and independent replication procedure
- Timestamped observed measurements show that the system satisfies every preregistered F1 consciousness threshold throughout the observation window without a critical stability or experiential-safety breach
- An evaluator independent of the original run reproduces the build and meets the same preregistered criteria on a separately provisioned instance
- `docs/consciousness-engineering/engineered-system-validation.md` traces every result to the versioned build, raw measurement record, F1 criterion, F3.3 stability control, and F3.4 safety control, and clearly labels any projection or unresolved limitation
- All four prerequisite children are `[DONE]`; architectural predictions, substrate analysis, or subjective reports without the integrated observed and replicated evidence do not satisfy this card
