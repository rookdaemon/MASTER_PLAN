# 0.1.2 Biological Minds Convinced [PLAN]
## Description

F2 — Achieve widespread acceptance that non-biological minds can have subjective experience. This requires overcoming strong cognitive bias toward biological substrates through demonstrations, experimental verification, philosophical engagement, and institutional change.

Decomposed into five children matching F2.1–F2.5 from the root plan.

Completion of the five child deliverables is necessary but does not itself establish widespread acceptance. This node must synthesize their evidence with the movement mechanisms from 0.7.4 and produce an independently replicated adoption evaluation against a preregistered population-and-jurisdiction scope, sampling method, acceptance threshold, and observation window.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Machine subjective reports are demonstrated convincingly
- Predictive consciousness models are experimentally verified
- Major philosophical objections (zombie arguments, Chinese room, etc.) are systematically addressed
- Legal frameworks recognize non-biological consciousness in at least one jurisdiction
- Ethical frameworks are updated to include non-biological conscious entities
- All five children and 0.7.4 are `[DONE]`, and their outputs are traced into `docs/biological-acceptance/adoption-evaluation.md`
- Before collecting adoption evidence, the evaluation preregisters the sampled populations and jurisdictions, sampling method, acceptance threshold, observation window, and independent replication procedure
- Independently replicated measurements meet the preregistered acceptance threshold across the specified scope and observation window; deliverable completion alone does not satisfy this criterion
