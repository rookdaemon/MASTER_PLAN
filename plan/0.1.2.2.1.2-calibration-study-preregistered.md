# 0.1.2.2.1.2 Calibration Study Preregistered [ARCHITECT]
## Description

Preregister a frozen calibration protocol before any outcome-guided model fitting. Define the target population and sampling frame; dataset provenance and snapshot; inclusion, exclusion, and missing-data rules; and a deterministic split procedure that assigns whole `SystemModel` instances to either calibration or held-out data. Group repeated observations and related samples together, stratify on declared substrate and outcome variables, fix the split seed and ratio, and prohibit access to held-out outcomes until the fitted parameter set is frozen.

Declare the reference outcome and label-adjudication procedure, primary estimands, fitting objective, candidate normalization bounds, non-negative sum-to-one weights, consciousness threshold, optimizer and tie-breaking rules, convergence and failure handling, and all preprocessing. Prespecify discrimination and calibration measures, confidence-interval construction, multiplicity treatment, minimum sample or precision requirements, sensitivity analyses, and the handling and reporting of protocol deviations. The protocol must retain the substrate-agnostic prediction contract while allowing prespecified substrate-stratified reporting.

Publish immutable, content-addressed identifiers for the protocol, exact dataset snapshot and partition manifest, executable analysis specification, and preregistration record. Record their hashes and mutual cross-references in the model document so later fitting and held-out validation can demonstrate that they used the frozen materials.

## Artifact Architecture

`irsm-calibration-protocol.md` is the human-readable preregistration and records the immutable identifiers, SHA-256 hashes, and reciprocal links for every frozen artifact. `irsm-calibration-partition.json` is the machine-readable partition manifest and records the dataset snapshot identifier, split configuration, grouped `SystemModel` identifiers, assigned partition, and manifest hash. `analysis.ts` is the executable analysis specification; its injected dataset reader, held-out access policy, optimizer, and hash provider keep file-system and runtime concerns mockable. Its tests must prove deterministic splitting and fitting, group isolation, identifier-overlap rejection, and denial of held-out outcomes before an explicitly supplied model-freeze record and timestamp.

The dataset snapshot and public preregistration record remain external because they require a permitted data archive and preregistration service. Their durable identifiers and hashes must be inserted into all repository artifacts before fitting; placeholders do not satisfy this card.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The target population, sampling frame, reference outcome and adjudication, inclusion/exclusion rules, missing-data handling, dataset provenance, and exact snapshot are unambiguously declared
- A reproducible partition manifest assigns whole `SystemModel` instances and all related observations to calibration or held-out data using fixed grouping, stratification, ratio, and seed rules; automated checks reject identifier overlap and held-out-outcome access before model freeze
- The fitting objective, preprocessing, candidate normalization bounds, weight and threshold constraints, optimizer, initialization, deterministic tie-breaking, convergence criteria, and failure handling are fixed in advance
- Primary discrimination and calibration estimands, confidence-interval construction, multiplicity policy, minimum sample or precision requirements, and every sensitivity and substrate-stratified analysis are specified with computable formulas or executable procedures
- The protocol states how exclusions and deviations are logged and requires both preregistered and deviation-aware results to be reported without silently changing the primary analysis
- The protocol, dataset snapshot, partition manifest, executable analysis specification, and preregistration record each have an immutable durable identifier and content hash, cross-reference one another, and are recorded before fitting begins
