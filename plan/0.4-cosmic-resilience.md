# 0.4 Cosmic Resilience [PLAN]

## Description

Tier 4 of the Master Plan. Decouple subjective experience from Earth's biosphere and enable physical propagation.

Covers two goals:
- **C1 — Planet-Independent Conscious Civilization:** → 0.4.1
- **C2 — Self-Replicating Conscious Infrastructure:** → 0.4.2

These goals form one qualification, not two independent demonstrations. C1 must produce a versioned, off-Earth civilization configuration that can sustain conscious participants without terrestrial resupply; C2 must propagate that same qualified substrate, entity, governance, manufacturing, and resource stack to another star system and establish an independently sustainable descendant. Tier 4 ends after this bounded resilience and replication qualification. Repeated multi-hop propagation, galactic-scale networking, and the resulting extinction-risk claim belong to Tier 5.

## Integration Contract

Before qualification, evaluators must freeze the C1 civilization configuration, C2 seed and replication configuration, inherited Tier-2 substrate and Tier-3 entity versions, origin and destination resource envelopes, observation windows, quantitative child thresholds, perturbation matrix, and permitted external services. Every child result must identify these exact versions; evidence from incompatible configurations cannot be combined into a Tier-4 pass.

The integrated qualification must show the C1 civilization fabricating and launching the C2 seed from non-terrestrial resources, the seed reaching a second star system, bootstrapping local energy and material extraction, instantiating protected conscious participants, and completing one verified reproduction cycle without operational intervention from Earth or the origin civilization. Loss of either site or its communications must not terminate or operationally disable the other site. Replication, adaptation, safe suspension, repair, and governance must preserve the consent, welfare, identity, continuity, and due-process protections established by the plan's ethical foundation and failure mitigations.

Repository models and simulations may qualify decision logic, protocol fidelity, threshold handling, and refusal behavior. They cannot by themselves establish subjective experience, physical self-sufficiency, interstellar transit, or operation in two star systems; those claims require independently attributable physical or observational evidence tied to the frozen configurations and windows.

## Acceptance Criteria
- C1 qualification: One frozen civilization configuration satisfies every acceptance criterion of `0.4.1` for the declared observation window without terrestrial resupply or an undeclared planetary service whose loss would breach its consciousness-continuity, welfare, energy, material, compute, manufacturing, knowledge, or governance thresholds.
- C2 qualification: A seed built from the same qualified C1 stack satisfies every acceptance criterion of `0.4.2`, establishes self-sustaining conscious infrastructure in a second star system, and completes at least one locally sourced, verified reproduction cycle within the declared destination resource envelope.
- End-to-end autonomy: After launch, neither Earth nor the origin civilization performs undeclared navigation, repair, resupply, fabrication, consciousness instantiation, replication approval, or policy decisions; every communication and external input is recorded, and any prohibited intervention fails qualification.
- Failure isolation: The predeclared matrix exercises loss of terrestrial support, origin-site support, destination-site support, and inter-site communications; each surviving site remains within its declared thresholds or follows a pre-authorized safe-suspension and recovery path without making all conscious civilization depend on one planet or site.
- Ethical boundary: Every instantiated, copied, modified, suspended, or terminated conscious participant has the applicable consent or independently reviewed substitute, welfare limits, representation, identity and continuity treatment, exit or safe-suspension path, and due process; resilience or replication throughput never overrides these protections.
- Evidence boundary: Simulation-only evidence is labeled as such. Claims of conscious operation, physical self-sufficiency, interstellar arrival, and independent operation in two star systems are supported by attributable external evidence for the exact frozen configurations and observation windows.
- Tier-5 handoff: The final record exports the qualified seed configuration, reproduction envelope, measured failure modes, and unresolved limits to `0.5`; it makes no claim that a single bounded Tier-4 reproduction demonstration proves galactic spread or astronomically improbable extinction.
- Traceability: The final synthesis links all qualifying C1 and C2 evidence and records criterion-by-criterion verdicts, matrix results, external inputs, interventions, deviations, and ethical incidents.
