# 0.4 Cosmic Resilience [PLAN]

## Description

Tier 4 of the Master Plan. Decouple subjective experience from Earth's biosphere and enable physical propagation.

Covers two goals:
- **C1 — Planet-Independent Conscious Civilization:** → 0.4.1
- **C2 — Self-Replicating Conscious Infrastructure:** → 0.4.2

These goals form one qualification, not two independent demonstrations. C1 must produce a versioned, off-Earth civilization configuration that can sustain conscious participants without terrestrial resupply; C2 must propagate that same qualified substrate, entity, governance, manufacturing, and resource stack to another star system and establish an independently sustainable descendant. Tier 4 ends after this bounded resilience and replication qualification. Repeated multi-hop propagation, galactic-scale networking, and the resulting extinction-risk claim belong to Tier 5.

## Integration Contract

Before qualification, evaluators must freeze the C1 civilization configuration, C2 seed and replication configuration, inherited Tier-2 substrate and Tier-3 entity versions, origin and destination resource envelopes, observation windows, quantitative child thresholds, perturbation matrix, and permitted external services. Every child result must identify these exact versions; evidence from incompatible configurations cannot be combined into a Tier-4 pass.

The integrated qualification must show the C1 civilization fabricating and launching the C2 seed from non-terrestrial resources, the seed reaching a second star system, bootstrapping local energy and material extraction, instantiating protected conscious participants, and completing one verified reproduction cycle without operational intervention from Earth or the origin civilization. Loss of either site or its communications must not terminate or operationally disable the other site. Replication, adaptation, safe suspension, repair, and governance must preserve the consent, welfare, identity, continuity, and due-process protections established by the plan's ethical foundation and failure mitigations.

Repository models and simulations may qualify decision logic, protocol fidelity, threshold handling, and refusal behavior. They cannot by themselves establish subjective experience, physical self-sufficiency, interstellar transit, or operation in two star systems; those claims require independently attributable physical or observational evidence tied to the frozen configurations and windows.

## Qualification Architecture

A Tier-4 qualification coordinator composes, but does not replace, the C1 and C2 qualifications. It consumes immutable evidence through injected ports for child qualification, perturbation execution, ethical authorization and participant protections, evidence-provenance verification, the external-input ledger, and evidence storage. It must not read clocks, filesystems, processes, networks, or other environment-specific resources directly.

Each qualification request supplies `evaluatedAt`, observation-window bounds, and immutable IDs plus content digests for the C1 civilization configuration, C2 seed configuration, inherited substrate and entity versions, origin and destination resource envelopes, threshold registry, perturbation matrix, and permitted-service policy. Every observation supplies `observedAt`, the applicable identities and digests, source and collection-method identities, site, raw-evidence digest, custody or attestation record, and evaluator-independence disclosure. The provenance port must verify those fields against the frozen request without performing ambient network or filesystem access. The coordinator rejects future-dated or out-of-window evidence, mismatched configurations or digests, unverifiable provenance, undisclosed evaluator conflicts, circular reliance on a child verdict as its own physical evidence, missing matrix scenarios, threshold violations, undeclared inputs or interventions, and absent ethical authorization or participant-protection records.

The frozen threshold registry must assign a unit, comparison operator, and finite numeric value to the C1 observation duration; consciousness-continuity and welfare limits; energy, material, compute, manufacturing, repair, knowledge, and governance service levels; locally replaceable critical-component coverage; C2 transit deadline; destination bootstrap deadline; reproduction-cycle deadline; and recovery limits for every perturbation. Prohibited terrestrial and origin-site operational inputs have a maximum of zero after their declared cutoffs. The required site count is two independent star systems and the required locally sourced destination reproduction count is at least one.

The coordinator emits an immutable record containing the request identities, child verdicts, site and reproduction evidence, every perturbation result, external inputs, interventions, deviations, ethical incidents, criterion-level reasons, and an overall pass or failure. Tests are written first with in-memory ports and explicit timestamps. Passing repository tests establishes orchestration and refusal behavior only, subject to the evidence boundary below.

## Behavioral Specification

Tests must be written before the coordinator and cover these externally observable scenarios:

- A request passes only when both child verdicts pass, all evidence matches the frozen identities and digests, observations fall within the supplied window, every matrix scenario passes or completes its pre-authorized recovery path within threshold, no prohibited input occurs, and ethical authorization and participant-protection records are complete.
- A configuration or digest mismatch, future-dated or out-of-window observation, missing matrix scenario, non-finite or absent threshold, threshold violation, missing child verdict, or incomplete site or reproduction evidence produces a failed immutable record with a criterion-level reason identifying the rejected field or scenario.
- Any terrestrial input after the C1 cutoff, or origin-site operational input after the C2 launch cutoff, violates its zero maximum and fails end-to-end autonomy even when both child verdicts report success.
- Missing, expired, or configuration-mismatched ethical authorization or participant-protection evidence fails the ethical criterion and overall qualification; child success cannot override it.
- Physical or observational evidence with a missing source, collection method, site, raw-evidence digest, custody or attestation record, or independence disclosure fails with a provenance reason; a child verdict cannot serve as the underlying evidence for the physical claim it asserts.
- Loss of either site or inter-site communications passes failure isolation only when the surviving site remains within all applicable thresholds or completes the declared safe-suspension and recovery path without undeclared intervention.

## Acceptance Criteria
- C1 qualification: One frozen civilization configuration satisfies every acceptance criterion of `0.4.1` for the declared observation window without terrestrial resupply or an undeclared planetary service whose loss would breach its consciousness-continuity, welfare, energy, material, compute, manufacturing, knowledge, or governance thresholds.
- C2 qualification: A seed built from the same qualified C1 stack satisfies every acceptance criterion of `0.4.2`, establishes self-sustaining conscious infrastructure in a second star system, and completes at least one locally sourced, verified reproduction cycle within the declared destination resource envelope.
- End-to-end autonomy: After launch, neither Earth nor the origin civilization performs undeclared navigation, repair, resupply, fabrication, consciousness instantiation, replication approval, or policy decisions; every communication and external input is recorded, and any prohibited intervention fails qualification.
- Failure isolation: The predeclared matrix exercises loss of terrestrial support, origin-site support, destination-site support, and inter-site communications; each surviving site remains within its declared thresholds or follows a pre-authorized safe-suspension and recovery path without making all conscious civilization depend on one planet or site.
- Ethical boundary: Every instantiated, copied, modified, suspended, or terminated conscious participant has the applicable consent or independently reviewed substitute, welfare limits, representation, identity and continuity treatment, exit or safe-suspension path, and due process; resilience or replication throughput never overrides these protections.
- Implementation contract: The test-first Tier-4 qualification coordinator uses only injected ports and caller-supplied timestamps; binds all evidence to the frozen configuration, resource-envelope, threshold-registry, perturbation-matrix, and permitted-service identities and digests; emits the complete immutable qualification record; and refuses every invalid or incomplete condition enumerated in the qualification architecture.
- Evidence boundary: Simulation-only evidence is labeled as such. Claims of conscious operation, physical self-sufficiency, interstellar arrival, and independent operation in two star systems are supported by external evidence for the exact frozen configurations and observation windows whose source, collection method, site, raw-evidence digest, custody or attestation record, and evaluator-independence disclosure pass the injected provenance verifier; circular self-attestation by the qualifying child is rejected.
- Tier-5 handoff: The final record exports the qualified seed configuration, reproduction envelope, measured failure modes, and unresolved limits to `0.5`; it makes no claim that a single bounded Tier-4 reproduction demonstration proves galactic spread or astronomically improbable extinction.
- Traceability: The final synthesis links all qualifying C1 and C2 evidence and records criterion-by-criterion verdicts, matrix results, external inputs, interventions, deviations, and ethical incidents.
