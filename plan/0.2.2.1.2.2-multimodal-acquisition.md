# 0.2.2.1.2.2 Multimodal Acquisition [PLAN]

## Description

Acquire complementary structural and molecular image volumes from an entire preserved brain at resolutions and throughput sufficient for the L2+ feature contract.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each required L2+ feature is mapped to a selected acquisition modality and measurable resolution.
- Instrument count, voxel volume, acquisition rate, raw data size, and end-to-end duration are dimensionally consistent.
- Section loss, imaging artifacts, and cross-modality registration have explicit error budgets.
- Acquisition is demonstrated across one complete biological brain at the declared specimen scale with provenance and integrity records.
