## Description

Turn the parent’s hybrid consolidation-and-distribution choice into a bounded strategy for acting before accelerating expansion makes destinations unreachable. Define cosmological assumptions, reachability and launch-deadline calculations, resource-allocation trade-offs, autonomous-seed requirements, and the ethical limits on creating permanently separated conscious civilizations.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `docs/cosmological-longevity/cosmological-horizon-strategies.md` compares pre-positioning, distributed redundancy, and speculative horizon bridging under an explicit cosmological model and declared uncertainty bounds

- Horizon bridging remains a research-only option unless its stated falsifiable physics and safety gates are met, and the artifact records residual uncertainty without counting it toward baseline survival
