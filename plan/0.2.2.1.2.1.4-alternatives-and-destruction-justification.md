# 0.2.2.1.2.1.4 Alternatives and Destruction Justification [PLAN]

## Description

Compare destructive and non-destructive preservation approaches against the same L2+ evidence contract and justify the selected protocol.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Plausible non-destructive and minimally destructive alternatives are evaluated using common fidelity, timing, scale, validation, and feasibility criteria.
- Each rejected alternative has an evidence-backed failure against at least one mandatory requirement rather than a preference-based dismissal.
- The selected protocol minimizes irreversible destruction among approaches that meet all mandatory preservation thresholds.
- The decision records residual uncertainty, conditions that would trigger reassessment, and implications for acquisition and consent.
